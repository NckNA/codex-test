# Report: 004-B.4 treatment plan patient preview

## What was implemented

- Added a read-only patient-facing treatment plan preview modal.
- Added the `Предпросмотр для пациента` action for each treatment plan in `TreatmentPlansTab`.
- Preview shows patient information, date, plan title, plan status, and `Врач: не указан`.
- Preview shows chief complaint text and related teeth.
- Preview shows findings linked to the selected plan through `stage.findingIds`.
- Preview shows treatment stages, teeth, descriptions, stage statuses, prices, and total plan amount.
- Preview shows additional recommendations and risk zones not included in the selected plan.
- Preview shows the required important note text.

## Storage usage

- Reads patient data through `storage.getPatients()`.
- Reads chief complaint through `storage.getChiefComplaint(patientId)`.
- Reads findings through `storage.getFindings(patientId)`.
- Does not write to storage.
- Does not add storage keys or migrations.
